unSpootify for Mac: first launch (one time only)
================================================

1. Drag "unSpootify" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
   (Don't open it from here: it would stop working once this window is closed.)

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click unSpootify.
   macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't check it for malicious software". Click "Done" (or "OK").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and next to
   "unSpootify was blocked" click "Open Anyway". Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

That's it. From now on it opens like any other app (you can keep it in the Dock).

If macOS asks whether "sockseek" may accept incoming network connections, click "Allow".
(sockseek is the part of unSpootify that talks to Soulseek.)

Didn't see "Open Anyway"? Ask whoever sent you the app; there's a one-line fix.
