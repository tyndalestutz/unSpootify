**Turn your Spotify playlists into a music library you actually own.**

> 🚧 Being rebuilt as a proper Mac and Linux app. Not ready to use yet.

## How it'll work

1. **Install:** open the download and drag unSpootify into Applications.
2. **Grab your playlist:** click *Get my playlist*, log in to Spotify, export.
3. **Download:** drop the playlist in, pick a folder, hit Download.

That's it. No terminal, nothing else to install.
